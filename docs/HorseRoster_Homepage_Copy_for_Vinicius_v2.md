Section 2 - A better way to present horses online
HorseRoster gives every horse a clean, professional profile with the photos, video, and details buyers
actually want to see. Trainers/Barns get a roster page to showcase available horses in one place, while
buyers get a simpler way to discover, review, and follow horses they are interested in.
Section 3 - Built for modern horse sales
For buyers
Browse horses, review media and details, discover more horses from the same barn or trainer, and

For Trainers/Barns
Create a professional roster, present horses with more clarity, manage horse documents in EquiVault, and

Professional horse profiles
Each horse gets its own dedicated page with media, details, and a polished presentation that feels far

Barn and trainer rosters

EquiVault

Horse sales are still too fragmented. Buyers chase information across text messages, Instagram,

brings everything into one modern, professional experience.
